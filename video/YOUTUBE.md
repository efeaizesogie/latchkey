## Description (paste as is; replace the bracketed parts after the mainnet deploy)

```
Latchkey is a self-custodial vault with a latch. Check in now and then. If you go quiet for longer than you chose, a grace period starts; if you still don't check in (or two of your guardians confirm), the latch trips and everything in the vault goes to the address you named: your own backup wallet after a lost phone, or your heir.

The latch is a TapeOut circuit on X Layer: 18 NAND + 2 LATCH, built by REF on a second taped-out circuit that counts guardian votes. Every check-in, poke and trip is one step() of that circuit, and once the latch trips it can never be undone. Notices go out over TapeSend from Latchkey's own circuit container, and the app is a single file built for X Layer DeWEB.
```
